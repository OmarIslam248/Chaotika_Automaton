# Design Process

## Target user
Children aged 4–12 who like building and tinkering. Goal: spark interest in mechanical design and hands-on making.

## Options considered

| Option | Idea | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Lumberjack | Lumberjack chops a tree, butterflies fly off with each swing | Rejected: too many moving parts for a child to build, and an axe creates a finger-pinch hazard |
| 2. Car jack | Jack lifts heavy objects with little input | Rejected: monotonous, a utility rather than a toy, only 1–2 moving parts, exposed pinch/trap points |
| 3. Chaotic Animal Fire Scene | Dog barks at a burning tree while a predator bird abandons its prey | **Selected** unanimously: appealing to kids, simple cams and followers, rounded and safe parts |

## Phases

1. **Meetings and drawings** – finalize dimensions, concept sketches, engineering drawings.
2. **SolidWorks parts and assembly** – model each part, assemble, check function.
3. **Prototype construction** – shell with side cutout for the cams, layered dog figure, cam and follower alignment, actuation system, rotating fire.
4. **Additional elements** – predator and prey birds on the follower rods. The predator starts in pursuit but ends up moving opposite to the prey, which reinforces the "irrational chaos" theme.
5. **Final evaluation** – requirement check: 6 independently moving parts (needed 5), 3 dependent parts, surface size within the project limit.

## Mechanism notes

- Circular cams are mounted 5 mm off-center on the axle, producing vertical follower displacement.
- Rectangular followers connect to rods with horizontal copper extensions that attach to the tail and tongue, converting vertical displacement into rotation about the pivot.
- The fire is spun by a perpendicular cam/follower pair using friction at the tangent surfaces instead of bevel gears.

## Ethics and safety

- Child-safe, non-toxic materials; no sharp edges, pinch points, or small detachable parts.
- Fire is shown without glamourizing it; the scene should support basic fire-safety awareness.
- No violence, injury, or trauma depicted. Clear age labelling.
- No manipulative marketing aimed at children.
